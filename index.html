About the Workshop

What is ANZWEE?

The annual Australia New Zealand Workshop on Experimental Economics (ANZWEE) aims to provide researchers interested in experimental and behavioural economics with an opportunity to present their latest research, receive constructive feedback in an informal environment, and network with other researchers.

ANZWEE 2026 is the 19th edition of the workshop, hosted by the Department of Economics at the University of Otago's Otago Business School. We welcome submissions from researchers at all career stages working in experimental and behavioural economics.

Schedule

Programme

Workshop sessions will run across Monday 16 and Tuesday 17 November 2026. Depending on the number of submissions, an early finish on Tuesday and/or later start on Monday may be considered.

Sunday 15 November — Pre-Workshop Social Activity

A pre-workshop social activity will be held the evening before the workshop begins. Details to be confirmed — watch this space.

Monday 16 November — Workshop Day 1

Full day of paper presentations. All presenters are invited to a workshop dinner on the evening of Monday 16 November. Please note the dinner is exclusively for presenters.

Tuesday 17 November — Workshop Day 2

Continued paper presentations. Final schedule will be circulated to registered participants closer to the workshop date.

Participation

Attendance & Registration

ANZWEE 2026 will be held in-person only. There is no workshop fee for ANZWEE 2026. However, we request all participants — including non-presenters — to register to confirm their attendance.

Submit a Paper Register as Attendee

Call for Papers

Submit a Paper

We welcome submissions of papers in experimental and behavioural economics at any stage of completion. The submission deadline is Monday 10 August 2026 (any time zone).

Your submission should include: your name, institution, email address; paper title, co-authors, and abstract; a few keywords; and whether you plan to attend the reception and dinner on Monday 16 November and/or the pre-workshop social activity.

Attendance

Register to Attend

All attendees — whether presenting or not — are asked to register so we can plan numbers. Registration is free. Please indicate whether you plan to attend the reception and dinner on Monday 16 November, and any dietary requirements.
